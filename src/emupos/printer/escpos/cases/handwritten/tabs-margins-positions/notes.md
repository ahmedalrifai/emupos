# tabs-margins-positions

Hand-written. Tab positions at columns 10 and 20 (ESC D) aligning a small table with HT; 4 dots
of right-side character spacing (ESC SP); a 48-dot left margin (GS L) with a 192-dot print area
(GS W) and centred text inside it; then, back on the full width, `A`, `B` at the absolute
position 300 (ESC $) and `C` 24 dots to the right of `B` (ESC \\).
Spec: receipt-printer, "Supported print commands" (scenario "Tabs align columns").
